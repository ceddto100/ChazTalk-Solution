Straight answers

Questions I get asked.

How fast do I see results?

Slower than anyone selling you a dream will admit. New sending domains need about two weeks of warm-up before real volume goes out, so the first month is mostly build and ramp. First replies usually land in weeks three and four, and the first meetings typically follow shortly after. Anyone promising booked meetings in week one is either burning your domain or making it up.

Is cold email even legal?

Business-to-business cold email is legal in the US under CAN-SPAM, and I run it the way the law requires: accurate sender information, a real physical address, a working opt-out honored immediately, and no misleading subject lines. I don't do cold texting or cold calling — those fall under much stricter rules, and the risk isn't worth it to you or to me.

Will this wreck my company's email domain?

Not if it's built properly, which is most of what the setup fee pays for. Outreach never goes out from your primary domain — I register separate lookalike domains, warm them gradually, keep daily volume inside safe limits, and monitor deliverability. Your main domain stays clean.

What do you actually need from me?

An hour up front to explain what you sell, who your best customers have been, and what makes you different. Then approval on the target list and the messaging before anything sends. After that, show up to the meetings.

Do I own the list and the data?

Yes. The contact list, the campaign data, the replies, and any landing page built for you are yours, and that's in writing. If we part ways you leave with all of it.

What if the messaging isn't working?

Then we change it — that's the job, not a failure. Campaigns get read weekly: which segments reply, which subject lines land, which angle gets ignored. Underperforming sequences get rewritten and retested rather than left running to burn through your list.

What if I want out?

Month to month after setup. Cancel with 30 days' notice and you keep the list, the data, and the landing page. No cancellation fee, no hostage situation.
